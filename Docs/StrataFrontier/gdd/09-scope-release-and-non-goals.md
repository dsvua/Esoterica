# Scope, Release, and Non-Goals

## Development envelope

- Solo developer
- Part-time development
- No hard release deadline
- Commercial indie destination
- Prototype and technical-risk gates before production content

The complete vision is a long-term direction, not the first build. Roadmap gates decide when systems earn further investment.

## Platform and product

- Windows 11 PC first
- Mouse and keyboard first
- DX12 is the only initial renderer requirement
- Vulkan remains a protected future path, not prototype scope
- Premium one-time purchase
- No microtransactions, premium currency, battle pass, or paid power
- Substantial expansions considered only after a complete 1.0

## Release path

1. Private technical prototypes
2. Small closed playtests
3. Public demo after the core loop is coherent
4. Paid Early Access only after the colony loop is enjoyable, performance limits are disclosed, and public save compatibility is supportable
5. Complete 1.0

Internal prototypes may deliberately break saves and content schemas. Compatibility migrations, polished onboarding, and public stability guarantees become priorities near public release.

Early Access has no public mod loader, scripting API, mod-compatibility promise, or mod tools. Internal data-driven extensibility remains mandatory. Public save migrations follow the release policy above. Public modding may be reconsidered after the core game stabilizes.

## Engine commitment

Esoterica Engine is the fixed starting host. The project will not switch automatically. Wicked Engine remains a researched alternative, but migration requires an explicit user request.

## Performance expectation

Initial target: 1080p/60 FPS on a reasonable mid-range Windows gaming PC with one detailed settlement of roughly 50 colonists and background sites active. Exact hardware SKUs and chunk sizes are benchmark-derived.

The game has no hard population or colony cap. It displays simulation timing and capacity warnings. If the player deliberately builds beyond their hardware’s capacity, effective time slows; consequential simulation is not silently skipped.

## Explicit non-goals

- Multiplayer, cooperative play, or competitive play
- Console/controller-first UI during initial development
- First-person or third-person pawn possession
- Separate colony, caravan, battle, or encounter maps
- Empire-building as the central fantasy
- Forced campaign victory
- Photorealistic character production
- Modern electricity, modern industry, or automatic firearms in the initial tree
- Full Dwarf Fortress historical person simulation
- Full fluid dynamics, atmospheric CFD, or microscopic chemistry
- Universal per-object physics or one entity per grain
- Arbitrary wealth-scaled raids, arbitrary death-on-downed, or armor chip damage
- Runtime cloud AI as a gameplay dependency
- Public modding during Early Access
- Automatic engine updates or engine switching
- Slavery, forced prisoner labor, torture, or sexual violence

## Deferred until justified

- Playable nonhuman species
- Vulkan/Linux shipping support
- Public scripting/modding
- Dedicated audio middleware such as FMOD
- Effekseer adoption beyond its integration gate
- Complex genetic simulation
- Detailed economic finance
- Tides, ocean-current physics, and advanced erosion
- Full settlement-governance simulation
- Translation and polished onboarding
